# Contributing to Ibex

Small pull requests are welcome. Before submitting one, ensure that you have
the right to contribute the code and that it is not subject to terms that
conflict with the project licenses.

## Contributor License Agreement

Contributions cannot be accepted until you have signed the Ibex Contributor
License Agreement in [.github/CLA.md](.github/CLA.md). A CLA bot will ask you
to sign on your first pull request by commenting on the PR, and the PR check
stays red until you do. One signature covers your present and future
contributions.

In short, the CLA is a license, not a copyright transfer: you keep ownership
of your contribution, and you grant Bob Jansen the right to distribute it as
part of Ibex — under AGPL-3.0-only and under separately negotiated commercial
licenses — together with a patent license covering the contribution. The
operative terms are those in the CLA itself, not this summary. It is required
so that Ibex can remain AGPL-licensed while Bob Jansen retains the option to
offer commercial licenses for Ibex. If you cannot agree to it, please do not
submit the contribution.

## Attribution

Material contributors may be added to [CONTRIBUTORS.md](CONTRIBUTORS.md).
Third-party code requires its original license and attribution to be preserved.
